# Pitch v2 — 4.3.4 satellites (Y9 Environmental Sustainability)

Revised after adversarial reviews by Fable 5.2 and Codex GPT-6.2-Astra (v1: `4.3.4-satellite-pitch.md`, Codex review: `4.3.4-review-codex.md`). Both reviews converged on the same three fixes.

## Core change
v1 rewarded picking the "right" sensor and spotting a hot year. Neither is *evaluating* climate impact. v2's core skill is **judging how strongly satellite evidence supports a climate claim**, with ground-truth that can overturn your prediction.

## Lesson shape (50 min, standalone, direct classroom URL)
| Min | Part |
|---|---|
| 5 | Launch: "a satellite never photographs temperature — so what does it measure?" |
| 8 | **Sensor lab**: 4 quick interactions, one per variable, showing the raw signal → derived quantity (IR brightness → skin temperature; radar echo delay → height; reflected light → tree cover; microwave emission → ice concentration). Trade-offs (coverage, revisit, resolution, cloud), not right/wrong. Real footprints: altimeter ground tracks with gaps, a cloud gap with a quality flag, then the gap-filled product. |
| 20 | **Reef investigation (the game)**: GBR, Degree Heating Weeks over several summers. Predict which of two reefs bleached → **one survey flight** to spend → AIMS aerial survey reveal can overturn the prediction → buoy at a labelled depth vs satellite skin temperature. |
| 10 | **Three short transfer cases (~3 min each), each built on a trap**: Sea level (global 30-yr series showing acceleration ~2.1 → ~4.7 mm/yr vs a local tide gauge; one cherry-picked pair of years points the "wrong" way); Forest (Hansen loss pixels: fire perimeter vs clearing polygon — "which is climate, which is bulldozers?"; Black Summer = more fire-weather risk, not cause of each fire); Ice (Antarctic 2023 record lows vs Arctic September long-term decline — "is one record a trend?"; sea ice ≠ ice sheet, GRACE/ICESat-2 callout). |
| 7 | **Minister briefing (exit ticket)**: claim constructor — claim strength (overclaiming "proves" penalised as much as underclaiming), 2–3 evidence cards, 1 required limitation. Auto-scored; optional free text left to a teacher rubric; one-page teacher export. |

## Game rules
- First attempts score; predictions are scored on commitment, not correctness.
- Scarcity drives decisions: one survey flight, conflicting evidence.
- No ranks or shop. Polish goes into the two beats that are fun: visible sensor failures and predict → real-data reveal.
- End on use: warnings, reef management, Indigenous ranger fire mapping (Torres Strait/TSRA rangers, checked with the school's Aboriginal education contact; no deficit framing).
